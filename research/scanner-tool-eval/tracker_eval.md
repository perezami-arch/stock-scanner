# Scanner setups: do the time-series models filter them usefully?


**Method (point-in-time).** Input: `/workspace/tracker/setups-point-in-time-2026-10-08.csv` (canonical, read-only). The 846 main-sheet setups are primary and the 212 excluded (MCAP<$1.5B) are reported separately. `dup_not_counted` rows and rows with no alert date are skipped.
Cutoffs come from the file. US: history ends at the `cutoff_bar_date` close (the prior session for in-session, pre-market or date-only alerts; the 24.09 bot scan uses the given cutoff, though its data_asof_note says it used 22.09 closes). Crypto: 24h candles rebuilt from Yahoo hourly bars, ending at `cutoff_minute_utc` floored to the hour (date-only crypto = 00:00 UTC of the alert day). Perplexity task-list times (`ui_log`, 40 rows) are logged 5-70 min after the run, so the cutoff is capped at logged time minus 70 min. In practice this changed nothing: those stocks were in-session alerts that already used the prior close, and those crypto rows were already at 00:00 UTC.
Each tool forecasts a 20-bar path from up to 400 bars of history. Scores are direction-adjusted: SHORT flips the sign, so **keep = the tool agrees with the setup's direction**. For the rank-only factors, keep = the favourable half within the asset class.
Outcomes are direction-adjusted. Where present, the tracker's own day-5, day-20 and total columns are used (total = last available day ≤20; base = alert price). Rows without them, mainly the excluded set, are computed from prices after the cutoff (base = cutoff close). Excess returns subtract SPY (US) or BTC-USD (crypto) over the same window from the cutoff; there is also a total excess vs ETH for crypto. BTC setups have zero excess vs BTC by construction.
**Significance.** Setups on the same date share market moves, so iid p-values are optimistic. Read the date-clustered bootstrap CIs and the per-date RankIC t-stat (dates × asset class with ≥5 setups). Many tools × targets × subgroups are tested, so expect several nominal p<0.05 by chance.


## Key findings (summary written by hand; tables below are generated by report.py)
* **Sample:** 828 main-sheet setups (827 with the tracker's own outcomes) plus 200 excluded small caps. Tools: Kronos-mini (the strongest Kronos in the walk-forward; Kronos-small was stopped for CPU time), Chronos-2, TimesFM-2.5, momentum 12-1, low-vol 60d, and their blend.
* **No model is a useful filter.** Chronos-2 and TimesFM tend to point the wrong way: the setups they "keep" did worse than the ones they "reject." Total return, keep minus reject: Chronos-2 −5.1pp (clustered CI −8.6..−2.4), TimesFM −4.9pp (−8.3..−1.8), Kronos-mini −0.2pp (−3.0..+2.7).
  On excess vs SPY/BTC the gaps shrink: −1.3pp, −1.7pp and −2.2pp; only Kronos-mini's CI (−4.3..−0.1) excludes zero.
  Per-date RankIC is about 0 (|t| < 1.6) on total return. The models are mostly forecasting mean reversion while these setups trended.
* **Momentum 12-1 is the only tool that ranks setups usefully:**
  * total excess return: keep minus reject +3.1pp (CI +0.8..+5.3), per-date RankIC +0.16 (t 3.4);
  * day-20 excess: +3.6pp;
  * the momentum + low-vol blend: +2.8pp on total excess (CI +0.9..+5.1).
* **Value beyond the factors:** residual Spearman after removing momentum 12-1 and low-vol is negative for Chronos-2 and TimesFM on every target (e.g. day-20: −0.27 and −0.23) and about 0 for Kronos-mini. Adding any model to the factor blend made the keep/reject spread worse in all 9 cases (3 models × 3 targets).
* **Grok 'before breakout':**
  * In the old hold-no-stop CSV (41 longs, mean −2.83%, median −2.73%), no model would have filtered them out. Keep vs reject: Chronos-2 −2.64% vs −3.03% (p 0.88), TimesFM −4.02% vs −0.77%, Kronos-mini −3.08% vs −2.06%.
  * Only the momentum + low-vol blend separated them: kept −0.65% vs rejected −7.03% (p 0.037 iid, n 27/14), which is weak evidence.
  * In the canonical PIT set (69 prebreakout setups, longs and shorts, tracker outcomes ≤20 days), the average is about +0.6% total. There, Kronos-mini is a clear *anti*-filter on excess return: kept −2.75% vs rejected +5.10%, p 0.001.
* **Robustness:** the conclusions hold after removing IOVA, NEAR-USD or XP, and after dropping the top-1 and top-3 contributing tickers (SOL, ETH, LINK / DEXE). On the main sheet and in Grok crypto, the momentum and blend spreads stay positive and Chronos-2 and TimesFM stay negative. Perplexity (n≈110) is too small to say.
* **Data caveat:** crypto 24h candles are rebuilt from Yahoo hourly bars, which have gaps and ~50% zero-volume hours. That affects the volume input to Kronos only. Alpaca minute data was available but not used, since daily-bar models don't need minute cutoffs (noted as a possible refinement).

Setups scored: main sheet 828 (after dropping dup_not_counted and rows with no cutoff/price), excluded MCAP<1.5B 200. Realized source (main): {'tracker': 827, 'computed': 1}; excluded: {'computed': 200}.
