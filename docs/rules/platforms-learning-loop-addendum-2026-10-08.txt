RULE 1 - FRESH RESEARCH AT EVERY DECISION ("everything is based on repeated research at every decision")
- Decision points: entry scan, day-5 checkpoint, day-10 checkpoint, exit/hold decisions (stop hit, target hit, time stop).
- At each point every called platform researches FRESH with data available up to that moment (new news, X sentiment, price action,
  catalysts). A cached prior verdict is NEVER reused as the decision. (Reuse is allowed only to rebuild a report of the SAME scan moment,
  e.g. trial pass 2/3 on 08.10; such rows are marked reused=True and are not new decisions.)
- Each (re)research = a NEW dated verdict row linked to the trade: ledger /workspace/scan-runs/platform_verdicts_ledger.csv
  columns: trade_key (ticker|direction|entry_date), decision_point (entry|day5|day10|exit_check), asof_local, data_cutoff_utc, platform, model,
  prompt_version, verdict (approve/flag/veto) | conviction (Claude) | checkpoint_action, main_risk, cost_usd, fresh (True/False), skipped_reason, trial.
  The scoreboard measures every decision row, not only the entry.
- Checkpoint outputs: checkpoint_action in {hold, tighten, exit} = EXPERIMENTAL column only, weight 0, NO automatic action. Stops stay = approved 4xATR chandelier.

RULE 2 - FREE TECHNICAL RE-RUN AT EVERY CHECKPOINT (always runs, no LLM cost)

RULE 3 - COST GATE AT DAY 5 / DAY 10 (only open trades, batched)
- First Jev screens NEW Alpaca news per open position since the previous decision point (trusted headlines only, never X posts).
- Call Perplexity / Claude / GPT (and Grok) ONLY if at least one trigger:
    (a) Jev materiality >= 2 with confidence >= 0.8, or a relevant low-confidence headline;
    (b) price reached a decision level: within 1 ATR of the current chandelier stop, at/over Fib 1.618 tp_half, or a VA120 break;

- Otherwise write ledger rows with skipped_reason='skipped - no material change' (cost 0) so the scoreboard sees the decision was taken.
- Batch all open trades in one run; same budget guard (per-run cap; separate weekly cap).

RULE 6 - ONE RANKING, NO FIXED LONG/SHORT SPLIT (Ilan, 08.10 16:40)
- Every candidate that passes its rules is ranked on ONE score, longs and shorts together, and the top N (--candidates, default 20) are taken.
  That can be 20 longs and 0 shorts. No reserved slots per side.
- The score (unified_score, 0-100, same scale both sides): the 12-1 momentum percentile within the asset class
  (scanner_momentum.rank, LONG view), FLIPPED for shorts (momentum_score_short = 100 - long percentile). High means the momentum points
  the trade's way, so a short only outranks a long when its downside momentum is stronger than that long's upside momentum.
  Crypto uses the same percentile computed over the crypto universe scanned that day (crypto_scan.csv; too small a group for the stock
  percentile). Raw momentum_12_1 is only a fallback if the percentile could not be computed.
- Floor: --min-score (default 60). A name below it is not selected even if that leaves fewer than N.
  Why 60: on the validated sample (scanner-tool-eval, 139 stock setups with a day-20 outcome, 15 distinct entry days) the
  direction-adjusted own-price return to day 20 had a hit rate of 58.9% at score >= 60 vs 51.8% below it, with essentially the same
  mean (+2.08% vs +2.06%). The gap is carried by the LONG side; the short side of that sample is small and negative either way.
  Small sample, so the floor is a flag and is itself measured forward.
- Unchanged: shorts must pass the approved short rule (ck_score >= 3 and close < SMA200) to be candidates at all; the 2-per-sector cap
  counts per side ((asset, direction, sector), --sector-cap, 0 = off); the earnings block (5 trading days, strong-catalyst exception).
- candidates_full.csv lists EVERY rule-passing name with unified_score, score_rank, passes_min_score, selected and selection_note
  (נבחר / מתחת לסף / תקרת סקטור / נחסם - דוח / מחוץ ל-N הראשונים).
