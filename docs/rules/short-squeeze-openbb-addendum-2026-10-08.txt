PIT rules
- FINRA SI: a report is usable only if its estimated PUBLICATION date (settlement + 7 NYSE business days) < alert date.
  Never keyed to the settlement date.
- Reg SHO: files for trade dates < alert date. Only the CHANGE is used: short_svr_z20/z60 = z-score of the last-5-session mean
  short-volume ratio vs the stock's own prior 20/60 sessions (excluding those 5); short_svr_trend = r5 - 20d mean. The absolute
  ratio is not a column (includes market-maker hedging).
- Price components from bars complete before the alert: short_comp_trend = close > SMA50 and 5d avg vol > 20d avg vol;
  short_comp_breakout = close > max High of the prior 20 sessions.
- short_comp_si = SI % float >= 15 or DTC20 (SI / 20d avg volume) >= 5. short_squeeze_candidate = comp_si and (trend or breakout).
- Borrow fee / utilization: paid-only (Ortex, S3) -> short_borrow_fee_paid always null. IBKR public short-stock FTP
  (ftp3.interactivebrokers.com, user shortstock, usa.txt) is attempted every snapshot (short_ib_fee, short_ib_rebate,
  short_ib_available, short_ib_fee_chg, short_ib_avail_drop_pct, short_comp_borrow = fee +1pp or availability -30% vs the
  previous snapshot) - FORWARD ONLY. On 08.10 port 21 timed out from the box, so these are null (meta.json ibkr_status).
