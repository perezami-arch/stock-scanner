DEFINITIONS
1. Fib spiral (exp_spiral_*): plane x = bars, y = price/u, u = ATR14 at the bar that CONFIRMED H1 ("1 bar = 1 ATR";
   spirals are scale-dependent, this is a fixed convention, not optimised). Center H1, start L0, golden spiral
   r = r0*phi^(quarter turns), sweeping from L0 through the retracement side (below H1 for longs) into the future, 3 turns.
   Columns: arc above/below the close on the last bar, distance to nearest arc (ATR), near_arc (<=0.5 ATR), next quarter-turn
   node after the last bar (date, price, bars), next date the spiral crosses the current close.
   Note: golden spirals grow fast (x6.85 per turn), so nodes are often far away in price - that is the method, not a bug.
2. Gann (exp_gann_*): box = L0 -> H1 (range R, D bars). Price levels inside the box at 1/8 steps + 1/3, 2/3; boxes +/-2
   around it at 0,1/4,1/3,1/2,2/3,3/4. Time divisions D*(m+f) forward; a fraction is used only if its step is >= 1 bar.
   Angles 1x1 / 1x2 / 2x1 from L0 (up) and from H1 (down), swing-based scale 1x1 = R/D per bar (exp_gann_scale_atr shows
   that slope in ATRs). Columns: nearest level and angle above/below the close, next time division date, next whole box.
3. Volume profile (vp60_, vp120_, vpanc_ from L0, vp20m_): daily volume spread uniformly over High-Low into 50 bins;
   POC, value area 70% (grown from the POC), HVN/LVN = local max/min of the [1,2,1]-smoothed profile (>= mean / <= 0.6 mean),
   distances in ATR14, position vs value area. vp20m = Alpaca 1-min bars over the last 20 visible daily bars (stocks
   regular session, split-adjusted, SIP feed; crypto 24h), typical price, 100 bins; ~2-4 s per ticker, cached in
   /workspace/scanner-geometry-cache/alpaca_1min/. Daily profiles use Yahoo adjusted prices; vp20m uses traded prices.
4. Candles (candle_*): own vectorised pandas (TA-Lib not installed). Rules in scanner_geometry.CANDLE_RULES:
   doji body<=10% range; dragonfly/gravestone; hammer/hanging man (lower shadow >= 2x body, upper <= 25% range, body
   10-35%), inverted hammer/shooting star; marubozu body>=90% range; spinning top; engulfing; harami; piercing; dark cloud;
   morning/evening star; three white soldiers/black crows; tweezer top/bottom (+/-0.1 ATR); inside/outside bar.
   Context: 10-bar least-squares slope of Close before the pattern x9/ATR14: <=-1 downtrend, >=+1 uptrend.
   Volume confirmation: last-bar volume / mean of 20 prior bars >= 1.2. Frequencies on 2,687 real bars: inside bar 16%,
   doji 12%, engulfing ~1.8% each, morning star 0.45% (candle_pattern_frequency.csv).

5. LIQUIDITY ZONES (liq_*, added 08.10 13:55 at Ilan's request) - objective rules in scanner_geometry.liquidity():
   Swings = 3-bar fractals (High above the 3 bars on each side, Low mirror), CONFIRMED 3 bars later, last 120 bars.
   Pools: untaken confirmed swing highs above the close = buy-side (liq_bsl_*), swing lows below = sell-side (liq_ssl_*).
     Adjacent levels within 0.25 ATR14 (cluster span <= 0.5 ATR) form one pool = equal highs/lows (touches >= 2).
     A swing is "taken" once a later High exceeds it by > 0.25 ATR (the same tolerance), so a hair-thin wick does not
     erase equal highs. Output: level, touches, age in bars, distance in ATR, nearest pool + strongest pool within 5 ATR.
   Standard levels: prior bar (day) high/low, prior complete week and month high/low before the alert week/month.
   Sweeps (last 3 CLOSED bars): High above a pool known the bar before (or above the prior bar high) and Close back below
     = bearish sweep (buy-side liquidity taken); mirror = bullish sweep. Date, level and kind are logged.
   Fair value gaps: bullish when Low[k] - High[k-2] >= 0.1 ATR (zone High[k-2]..Low[k]), bearish mirror; zone shrinks with
     partial fills and disappears when filled; nearest open gap above/below the close (last 120 bars).
   Order blocks: displacement bar with body >= 1.5 x ATR14 that closes beyond the last confirmed swing high (bull) / low
     (bear) = break of structure; OB = last opposite-colour candle within the 5 bars before it (zone = its Low..High);
     unmitigated = price never traded back into the zone after the displacement; nearest above/below (last 250 bars).
   Volume-profile link: liq_*_vp_node = HVN/LVN of the 60/120-bar daily profile within max(0.25 ATR, 1 bin) of the pool;
     liq_*_hvn = True when it is an HVN.

CONFIRMATION DATES (the researcher's rule - every object counts only from the close of the bar that confirms it)
   exp_anchor_origin_confirmed_at / exp_anchor_end_confirmed_at: bar on which the 2xATR zigzag confirmed each anchor pivot
     (spiral, Gann box/angles and the anchored volume profile). Example NVDA: swing end 04.09, confirmed 10.09.
   Pools: liq_*_first_confirmed_at (first swing confirmed), liq_*_equal_confirmed_at (SECOND swing confirmed = equal
     highs/lows exist from here), liq_*_confirmed_at (last member confirmed = current touch count); same for *_strong_*.
   Sweep: liq_sweep_confirmed_at = the closed sweep bar. FVG: liq_fvg_*_confirmed_at = close of the 3rd bar.
   Order block: liq_ob_*_confirmed_at = close of the displacement (break-of-structure) bar.
   Prior day/week/month: liq_pd/pw/pm_confirmed_at = close of the last bar of that period.
   Tests: every confirmed_at <= last visible bar; equal highs dated at 2nd swing + 3 bars, FVG at the 3rd bar, OB at the
     displacement bar (synthetic checks), and the 88-cutoff look-ahead check covers all ~158 columns.

LIMITATIONS
  - Only the LONG view is wired in swing_scan (as for Fib/Elliott); direction='SHORT' works in the module.
  - When no confirmed 2xATR swing exists, spiral/Gann/anchored profile are blank with geo_flag.
  - Small swings (3-6 bars) give tight Gann time grids; spiral/Gann are conventions with no evidence yet.
  - Crypto is not in swing_scan; geometry_columns works on crypto frames (tested BTC/ETH) for the researcher/annotate.
  - US names below $1.5B (or unverified mcap) are not computed in the scan.
  - Liquidity is built from daily bars only (no intraday swings, no order-book data). Crypto liquidation heatmaps
    (Coinglass and similar) are NOT included - a possible paid data source for later, needs approval and a key.
  - SMC concepts (pools, sweeps, FVG, OB) have many competing definitions; ours are fixed objective versions, untested.
  - The 1-min profile costs ~3 s per new ticker (cached after); the scan caps it at 300 s (SCAN_GEOM_INTRADAY_BUDGET_S).
