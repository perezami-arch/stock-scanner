POINT-IN-TIME CUTOFF (same as backtest)
- US stocks: alert before 16:00 ET -> previous session close 16:00 ET; at/after 16:00 ET on a session day -> that day 16:00 ET. Sessions = SPY bars.
- Crypto: the alert minute, or 00:00 UTC when only the date is known.

LIMITATIONS
- Alpaca news = Benzinga feed. History reaches ~2015 but is thin early (NVDA 1-9.1.2016: 5 articles; SBUX 1-8.1.2024: 3) -> counts are not
  comparable across years; fine for the 2026 setups. Filter is on created_at; later headline edits (updated_at) are not undone.
- Counts include multi-ticker roundups (use news_count_7d_focused). ETF news is mostly roundups.
- Crypto: BTCUSD/ETHUSD covered (99 / 38 articles in 7d on 8.10); small alts likely sparse. TASE: none (flag) - needs another source.
- 60d rate beta is noisy (|corr| often <0.2 for single stocks); the 8.10 window had +44bp in 20d, so signs reflect this regime (e.g. banks negative).
- AAII needs a manual weekly cache refresh; aaii_pit marks stale when the latest week is >8 days old (macro_regime_pit.json).
